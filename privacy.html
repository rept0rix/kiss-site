Privacy Policy

For the KISS mobile app and related services · Effective October 5, 2026

KISS lets people find friends, send and receive kisses, and share a profile. This policy explains what the app processes and how to contact us about your information.

Information the app processes

  • Phone number and profile: your phone number is used as an account identity. If you provide a name or profile photo, the app can store it with your profile.
  • Contacts: when you choose Import Contacts, KISS reads contact names and phone numbers from your device to look for people already using KISS. Contact details used for matching are sent to KISS's service. The app may keep imported entries on your device so you can use them again. You can also enter or upload contact details yourself instead.
  • Photos: profile and kiss-card photos you choose may be stored and shown to people you interact with.
  • App activity and content: KISS stores sent and received kiss events, short notes you add, friend and block choices, and profile settings so the app can provide inbox, orbit, and social features.
  • Optional social sign-in: if you connect Google or X, the identity provider and KISS's sign-in service process account identifiers and profile details needed to sign you in.
  • Technical data: our hosting and database providers may process routine request and security logs, such as device/network information, to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts, match contacts, deliver kisses and invitations, display profiles and activity, keep the service working, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. The app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

Where information is processed

Information needed for online features is processed by the services that host and operate KISS, including its application hosting and database providers. Google or X also process information when you choose their sign-in option. These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices and deletion

Contacts access is controlled by your device settings. You can use KISS without importing contacts. You can change profile details in the app. To ask us to access, correct, or delete account information, email na0ryank0@gmail.com from the account address or include the phone number associated with your account. We may need to verify the request. Some records may remain in protected backups for a limited period or where required for security or law.

Children

KISS is a social app intended for people old enough to use its social and contact features under the laws where they live. It is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as KISS changes. The effective date above will be revised when we do. For privacy or support questions, visit KISS Support or email na0ryank0@gmail.com.
